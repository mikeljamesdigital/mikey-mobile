What's Included in Our $499 Summer Boat Prep Package

Every component of our comprehensive package addresses critical maintenance needs for inboard and V-drive boats. Here's exactly what you get:

🛢️ Premium Synthetic Oil Change

Up to 8 quarts of high-quality synthetic oil engineered to handle extreme temperatures and extended runs. Unlike conventional oil that breaks down quickly in heat, synthetic formulations from trusted brands like Valvoline Marine or Mobil 1 Marine maintain viscosity and protective properties even when your engine runs hot.

Why it matters: Central Valley heat can push oil temperatures beyond 250°F. Synthetic oil's superior thermal stability prevents sludge buildup and keeps your engine protected during those long summer days on the water.

🔧 Premium Oil Filter Replacement

A new, high-efficiency oil filter traps contaminants, metal particles, and combustion byproducts that accumulate over time. Clean oil circulation is essential for engine longevity—a quality filter ensures every drop of that premium synthetic oil stays pure.

Why it matters: Even the best oil can't protect your engine if it's contaminated. Fresh filters provide maximum filtration efficiency right when your boat needs it most.

⚙️ V-Drive Oil Change

Fresh fluid for your V-drive system keeps gears meshing smoothly and prevents excessive wear. V-drives operate under significant stress, especially during acceleration and towing—clean fluid with proper additives protects these expensive components.

Why it matters: V-drive repairs can cost $2,000-$5,000. Regular fluid changes are cheap insurance against catastrophic failure.

🔄 Transmission Oil Change

New transmission fluid ensures smooth shifting and prevents wear during towing, docking, and high-speed operation. Marine transmissions face unique challenges from constant load changes—fresh fluid maintains optimal performance.

Why it matters: Transmission problems can strand you on the water or require expensive haul-outs. Preventive maintenance keeps you moving smoothly.

💧 Impeller Replacement

The most critical component of this package. Your impeller pumps cooling water through the engine—when it fails, overheating follows within minutes. We replace your worn impeller with a fresh, high-quality unit designed for your specific engine.

Why it matters: Impeller failure is the leading cause of boat engine damage in warm climates. Rubber blades deteriorate over time, especially in hot conditions. A $50 part can prevent a $10,000 engine rebuild. Learn more about impeller maintenance from Boats.com.
