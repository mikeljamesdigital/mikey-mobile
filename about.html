OUR STORY

From the Driveway
to Your Driveway

My journey into the automotive world started long before Mikey Mobile became a reality. Growing up in the Central Valley, I watched my father meticulously care for our family's vehicles — teaching me that regular maintenance wasn't just a chore, it was an act of responsibility for the people you love.

After years working in traditional auto shops and dealerships, I grew frustrated watching customers burn through their PTO or waste a Saturday morning for a routine 45-minute service. The process was broken — inefficient, inconvenient, and often intimidating.

I knew there had to be a better way. Why force Fresno drivers to come to the shop when the shop could come to them? Mikey Mobile was born from that idea — combining dealership-level expertise with the personal care of a trusted neighborhood mechanic, delivered right to your door.

This isn't just a business to me. It's the culmination of a lifelong passion and a commitment to serving my community with integrity and innovation.
— Mike, Founder of Mikey Mobile
