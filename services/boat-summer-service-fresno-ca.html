SERVICE BREAKDOWN

What We Do
& Why It Matters

Every item in the package serves a specific purpose. Here's exactly what we do and why each service is critical before summer.

01

Synthetic Oil Change (Up to 8 Qts)

We drain the old engine oil, replace it with premium synthetic marine oil (Valvoline or Mobil 1), and install a new OEM-quality oil filter.

Why it matters: Synthetic oil withstands extreme heat far better than conventional oil — critical for Central Valley summers. It also flows better at cold startup, reducing wear on every engine revolution.

02

V-Drive Oil Change

We drain the V-drive fluid, inspect it for metal particles that indicate internal wear, and refill with the manufacturer-recommended fluid specification.

Why it matters: The V-drive transfers power from your engine to the propeller. Contaminated or degraded fluid causes gear wear and can lead to a complete V-drive failure mid-season.

03

Transmission Oil Change

We replace the transmission fluid with fresh, manufacturer-specified fluid to ensure smooth gear engagement and reliable power transfer.

Why it matters: Old transmission fluid causes rough or delayed shifting and accelerates internal wear. A transmission rebuild is one of the most expensive repairs on a boat.

04

Impeller Replacement

We remove the old impeller, inspect the housing for damage or scoring, install a new OEM or premium aftermarket impeller, and verify cooling system flow.

Why it matters: The impeller pumps raw water through your engine's cooling system. A worn impeller causes overheating that can warp cylinder heads or crack engine blocks — the most catastrophic failure in marine engines.

05

Visual Inspection & Walkthrough

While we work, we keep an eye out for anything that looks off — hose wear, belt condition, fluid leaks, corrosion, or anything unusual. We flag it before you leave the driveway.

Why it matters: Catching a cracked hose or a loose fitting at home is a $20 fix. Catching it on the water is a tow bill and a ruined weekend. We give you a heads-up on anything we notice, no pressure.
